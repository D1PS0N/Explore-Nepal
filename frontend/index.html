Where to go

Top Destinations

From the Everest trail to the plains of Lumbini, handpicked places that define Nepal's landscapes and heritage.

🏞️
📍 Pokhara

Pokhara

Nepal's adventure capital — framed by Phewa Lake, Davis Falls, and paragliding launches above Sarangkot.

🛕
📍 Kathmandu

Kathmandu

The capital's UNESCO sites — Swayambhunath, Pashupatinath, Boudhanath, and Kathmandu Durbar Square.

🐘
📍 Chitwan

Chitwan

Jungle safaris, canoeing, and bird-watching through Chitwan National Park's dense lowland forest.

🏛️
📍 Bhaktapur

Bhaktapur

A medieval city frozen in time, home to Bhaktapur Durbar Square and the Nyatapola Temple.

🎨
📍 Lalitpur

Lalitpur

The city of fine arts — Patan Durbar Square, the Golden Temple, and centuries of Newari craft.

🏔️
📍 Solukhumbu

Solukhumbu

Gateway to the Himalayas — Everest Base Camp, Namche Bazaar, and Tengboche Monastery.

🏜️
📍 Mustang

Mustang

A high-altitude desert kingdom — Lo Manthang, Muktinath Temple, and the Kali Gandaki Gorge.

⛰️
📍 Manang

Manang

A trekking hub on the Annapurna Circuit, near Gangapurna Lake, Ice Lake, and Braga Monastery.

🪷
📍 Lumbini

Lumbini

The birthplace of Buddha — Maya Devi Temple, the Sacred Garden, and the World Peace Pagoda.

🏘️
📍 Ghandruk

Ghandruk

A Gurung village with the Gurung Museum and sweeping Annapurna viewpoints above the rooftops.

🏯
📍 Bandipur

Bandipur

A preserved hill town — Bandipur Bazaar, Tundikhel, and the limestone chambers of Siddha Cave.

🛕
📍 Janakpur

Janakpur

A sacred pilgrimage city centered on Janaki Temple, Ram Mandir, and Dhanush Sagar.

💧
📍 Rara Lake

Nepal's largest lake, tucked in remote Karnali — boating, hiking, and camping around Murma Top.

What to do

Popular Activities

Adventure at altitude or on the water — activities for every kind of traveler.

🥾

Trekking

Explore world-famous trekking routes including Everest Base Camp and Annapurna Circuit.

🪂

Paragliding

Enjoy breathtaking aerial views of Pokhara and the Himalayan mountain range.

🚣

Rafting

Experience exciting white-water rafting adventures on Nepal's beautiful rivers.

🐘

Jungle Safari

Spot rhinos, tigers, and exotic birds on a jeep or canoe safari through Chitwan National Park.

🚵

Mountain Biking

Ride rugged trails around Pokhara, Kathmandu Valley, and the hills of Bandipur.

🪢

Bungee Jumping

Take the leap at the Bhote Koshi Gorge, one of the highest bungee points in the world.

🎈

Hot Air Ballooning

Drift above Pokhara at sunrise for panoramic views of the Annapurna range and Phewa Lake.
